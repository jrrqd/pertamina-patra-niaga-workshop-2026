- `AGENTS.md`
- `knowledge-base/company/campaign_brief.md`
- `knowledge-base/company/brand_guideline.md`
- `knowledge-base/company/orgstructure.md`
- `knowledge-base/command-center.md`

- SOP yang disebut di profil itu
- `knowledge-base/company/sop/sop_sync_dan_status_report.md` (kecuali Anda CoS MRP yang memelihara papan)
